The Problem

A Valuable Resource Going to Waste.

Industrial facilities need water for many operational activities. Meanwhile, the condensate that air conditioning systems produce every day is usually just disposed of, so a potentially useful resource goes to waste while the facility buys water for tasks that may not need drinking-grade quality.

  • Potentially recoverable water is lost.Condensate leaves the building without ever being put to use.
  • Dependence on external supply.Facilities keep drawing on outside water for applications that may suit recovered water.
  • Availability and quality vary.Every site produces a different volume and a different quality of condensate.
  • Reuse needs proper evaluation.Decisions depend on quality assessment and operational review, not on guesswork.
  • One method cannot fit every facility.A cement plant, a hotel and a hospital each have different needs.

Why this matters in water-stressed regions such as Egypt

In countries like Egypt, where freshwater is limited and demand keeps growing, industry has good reason to look at every overlooked source. Recovering condensate will not solve water scarcity on its own. It is one practical step that lets a facility use less external water where the quality and the application allow it.

Our Solution

Meet Condensa: Smarter Water Recovery for Industry.

Condensa is an AI-powered IoT solution designed to collect air conditioning condensate, monitor the available quantity, assess selected water quality indicators, and support decisions about appropriate reuse opportunities.

  1. CollectCondensate water is gathered from suitable air conditioning systems.
  2. StoreThe collected water is held in a designated tank.
  3. MonitorSensors track water quantity and selected quality indicators.
  4. EvaluateReuse options are weighed against the facility's operational needs and water quality requirements.
  5. EstimatePotential water savings are estimated to support more efficient resource management.

One Water Source. Customized Solutions for Every Facility.

Condensa does not impose the same reuse application on every customer. Each facility is assessed individually to identify the reuse opportunities that suit it.

Technology and IoT Prototype

Where Water Recovery Meets Smart Technology.

The prototype combines connected hardware, monitoring, data analysis and, in future, AI capabilities. Each component has a clear job.

In circuit design

ESP32 microcontroller

The central controller. It reads the sensors and sends the data onward over Wi-Fi.

In circuit design

Ultrasonic water-level sensor

Measures the distance to the water surface, which indicates how much condensate the tank holds.

In circuit design

TDS sensor

Monitors total dissolved solids, an indicator of the minerals present. It supports an initial assessment only.

Optional

pH sensor

An optional addition that tracks the acidity of the collected water.

In circuit design

Temperature and humidity sensor

Records ambient conditions that relate to how much condensate a system may produce.

In circuit design

Relay module and solenoid valve

Let the controller switch a valve for suitable control functions, such as opening or closing an outlet.

The collection tank and its connected monitoring components complete the setup. The circuit shown in the next section is a simulation, so adjustable potentiometers stand in for the analog water-quality inputs.
